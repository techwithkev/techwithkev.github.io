How Do They Work?

Your Chatbot Today 🤖

Follows exact rules you wrote yourself using variables, loops, and conditionals. If the user input matches a condition, it gives the reply you coded.

✓ 100% Predictable · Never Hallucinates

The Class 2 LLM Chatbot 🧠

Trained on massive datasets instead of following fixed rules. It can respond flexibly to things it has never seen before — which is also why it can make mistakes or hallucinate.

⚡ Highly Flexible · Risk of Hallucination
