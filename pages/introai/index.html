Week 6 — Recommendation Systems

Be the Algorithm!

Activity 1: Read 5 student profiles with 8 parameters each and decide which movie to recommend — just like Netflix does.

Analyze Your Feed!

Activity 2: Reflect on how YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, and Netflix recommendations feel — and explore filter bubbles.

Design a Simple Recommender!

Activity 3: Build an interactive flowchart to design a board game recommender — drag nodes, connect decisions, and define outputs.

Knowledge Check Quiz

Activity 4: Multiple-choice quiz on content-based vs collaborative filtering, cold start, and filter bubbles.

Week 7 — AI Ethics & Data Privacy

Spot the Bias!

Activity 1: Analyze real-world AI scenarios to spot dataset bias, predict harms, and propose fairer solutions.

Personal Data Audit

Activity 2: Audit the apps and websites you use every week to uncover what data they collect and whether it's worth the trade-off.

Build Your Own Dataset

Activity 3: Design an AI dataset from scratch: choose a goal, define 4 features, build 5 sample rows, and audit for bias.

Data Detective

Explore anonymized datasets, identify data types, discover hidden patterns, and analyze what AI models could predict.

Week 8 — Advanced AI Ethics & Accountability

The Bias Card Twist

Exercise 1: Design a local AI solution, then adapt when an unexpected training data gap is revealed.

Bias Detective Stations

Exercise 2: Rotate through 4 case stations to investigate where AI bias comes from and who gets hurt.

Fix the Dataset

Exercise 3: Audit a skewed medical dataset and physically modify rows to make it truly representative.

Promise vs. Problem Placemat

Exercise 4: Evaluate real-world AI for Good tools (AlphaFold, Woebot, BeMyEyes, Ocean Cleanup) on potential and pitfalls.

Mini Ethics Trial

Exercise 5: Roleplay an AI healthcare company, affected patient, regulator, and judge in a high-stakes courtroom.

Week 9 — NPC Logic: Map Out Guard Logic

NPC Logic: Map Out Guard Logic

Design decision trees for a stealth-game guard NPC — mapping out how AI reacts to noise, sight, and losing the player.
