Prompt Writing Tips

Keep these in mind as you experiment today

🌟

Be Specific

Vague prompts give generic results. Add colours, materials, time of day, and emotions.

🎨

Name the Style

Words like "oil painting", "pixel art", "Pixar 3D", or "photorealistic" dramatically change the look.

💡

Set the Mood

Try adding "cinematic", "eerie", "joyful", "dramatic lighting" or "golden hour" to shift the feel.

🔁

Iterate & Refine

Don't stop at the first result. Tweak one element at a time and compare — that's how pros do it!
