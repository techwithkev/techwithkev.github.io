AI Posture Coach · Pose Estimation · Week 5 Activity 2

Week 5 · 20 Mins

Build an AI Desk Posture Coach

Instead of analyzing raw camera pixels, Pose Models extract 17 skeletal keypoints (shoulders, nose, ears, elbows) to detect human body position. In this 20-minute lab, you will train a real-time posture detector to spot when you're slouching or leaning into "phone neck"!

  • 1. Open Teachable Machine & choose Pose Project.
  • 2. Class 1: Good Posture (sit upright, shoulders relaxed, looking straight ahead). Record 30+ frames.
  • 3. Class 2: Slouching / Phone Neck (slouch in chair, lean head forward/down). Record 30+ frames.
  • 4. Click Train Model, export your shareable link, and test how accurately it spots your bad posture!
