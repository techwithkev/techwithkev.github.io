Interactive ML Training

Build and test your own machine learning classifier! Use Google's Teachable Machine to train a model based on Images, Audio, or Poses.

  • 1. Open Teachable Machine & click Get Started.
  • 2. Choose your project style: Image, Audio, or Pose.
  • 3. Define clear classes and collect 15-20+ varied examples per class!
  • 4. Click Train Model.
  • 5. Export the model and upload it to get your shareable link (follow the detailed steps below in Step 3).

📋 How to get your shareable link:

  1. In the Preview panel on Teachable Machine, click the blue Export Model button.
  2. In the modal that opens, ensure the Tensorflow.js tab is active.
  3. Click the blue Upload my model button (this makes it a public shareable URL).
  4. Once uploaded, copy the link under Your shareable link: (it looks like https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/models/xxxxxx/).
