🎮 Exercise Brief (~10 Minutes)

You are designing the AI brain for a stealth game guard. Your guard needs clear, consistent rules for three different situations — otherwise it will behave randomly and ruin the game experience.

Guard hears a suspicious noise nearby
👁️ Situation 2

Guard was chasing player but lost sight
