Create an AI Study & Presence Monitor

Real-world AI systems (like Tesla autopilot or driver attention monitors) constantly classify whether a human is attentive, distracted, or missing. In this activity, you'll train a 3-class Desk Guard to monitor your study session!

  • 1. Open Teachable Machine & choose an Image Project (or Pose).
  • 2. Class 1: At Desk / Studying (typing, taking notes, looking at screen — 25+ frames).
  • 3. Class 2: Away from Desk (empty chair, step out of frame — 25+ frames).
  • 4. Class 3: Distracted (looking at smartphone or turned away — 25+ frames).
  • 5. Train, export your shareable link, and test how reliably your AI guard tracks your focus.
