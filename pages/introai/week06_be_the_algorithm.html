You Are the Recommendation Engine

Below are 5 fictional student profiles — each with different tastes, habits, and behaviours. Your job: decide which movie to recommend to each student and explain why that data led you to that decision.

  • 🎯 Step 1 — Read each student profile carefully. Notice their genre preferences, watch habits, and mood patterns.
  • 🎬 Step 2 — Pick the best movie from the list for each profile. You must choose one!
  • 🔍 Step 3 — Identify the ONE data parameter that influenced your decision most.
  • 💬 Step 4 — Reflect on the whole class game: did everyone agree? What made it hard?
