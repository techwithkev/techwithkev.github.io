Your Turn! Practice Using the 5 Elements

Great prompts aren't just one sentence — they combine 5 key building blocks to give the AI crystal-clear guidance.

Activity Instructions:

  • 1 Read the scenario: Study each real-world situation and its required goals.
  • 2 Craft a prompt: Combine the 5 elements (Task, Context, Persona, Format, Tone).
  • 3 Test in your chatbot: Paste and run your prompt in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.
  • 4 Note down results: Record the output and briefly evaluate how well it adhered to constraints.
1. Task
What specific action must the AI perform?
2. Context
Background info, constraints & situation.
3. Persona
Who should the AI act or speak as?
4. Format
Email, dialogue, list, table, word limit?
5. Tone
Emotional mood: polite, humorous, eerie, etc.

Scenario 1 · Academic Communication

The Urgent Homework Extension Request 📄

The Situation:

You caught a severe flu the evening before your Grade 9 Physics lab report is due (tomorrow morning at 8:00 AM). Your teacher, Mr. Henderson, is famously strict about punctuality, but values honesty and proactive communication. You have already completed 75% of the lab data and calculations, but need 48 more hours to recover and type up your discussion section.

Target Element Constraints:

• Task: Request a 48-hour extension on the physics lab report
• Context: Due tomorrow 8 AM, sudden flu, 75% done
• Persona: Responsible, respectful high school student
• Format: Email with Subject Line, 2 short paragraphs & sign-off
• Tone: Courteous, humble, apologetic, and proactive

Scenario 2 · Creative Roleplay & Game Design

The Underground Fantasy Alchemist 🧙‍♂️

The Situation:

You are writing lore and NPC dialogue for a fantasy RPG video game. The adventurer walks into a shadowy, candle-lit curiosities shop hidden under a cobblestone alleyway during a violent thunderstorm. The shopkeeper is Pip, an ancient, eccentric goblin alchemist who speaks in whimsical riddles and rhymes, is obsessed with shiny glass beads, and sells bizarre magical concoctions with hilarious side effects.

Target Element Constraints:

• Task: Greet player in-character & list 3 wacky potions for sale
• Context: Underground shop during thunderstorm, Pip never breaks character
• Persona: Pip — ancient, eccentric, rhyming goblin alchemist
• Format: 1 paragraph dialogue greeting + 3 bullet points with [Name, Effect, Side Effect, Cost in trinkets]
• Tone: Whimsical, mysterious, quirky, slightly mischievous

Scenario 3 · Educational Analogy & Clarity

Explaining AI to a 5th Grader 🧠

The Situation:

Your 10-year-old cousin sees you playing with an AI tool and asks with wide eyes: "How does the computer actually know how to write stories and answer questions? Does it have a tiny human brain inside?" You want to engineer a prompt that instructs the AI to explain Machine Learning / how AI learns from patterns using a fun real-world analogy (like a master chef baking cookies or an athlete practicing basketball shots).

Target Element Constraints:

• Task: Explain how AI models learn from training data
• Context: Audience is a 10-year-old child; use a cooking or sports analogy; ZERO technical jargon
• Persona: Enthusiastic, friendly youth science mentor
• Format: Exactly 3 short paragraphs + 2-3 emojis + 1-sentence "Golden Rule" takeaway at the end
• Tone: Warm, cheerful, super clear, and encouraging
