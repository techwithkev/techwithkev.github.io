The Filter Bubble 🫧

What is a filter bubble?

When an AI only shows you content you already like or agree with, it can create a "filter bubble" — a personalised universe where you never see different viewpoints, genres, cultures, or opinions. This can feel great in the short term (it's all stuff you like!) but it might mean you're missing out on new ideas — or even being kept in an echo chamber.
