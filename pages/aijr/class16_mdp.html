The 5 Components of an MDP

S
States
The set of all possible situations (e.g. S1, S2, S3).
A
Actions
The choices available to the agent.
P(S'|S,A)
Transitions
Probability of reaching state S' given action A in state S.
R(S,A)
Immediate Reward
The immediate signal received right after an action.
γ
Discount Factor
How much future rewards are valued vs immediate ones.
