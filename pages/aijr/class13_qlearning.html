Key Exam Concepts:

  • Model-Free: Notice that Q-learning never asks for P(S'|S,A). It simply experiences a transition and updates based on the result.
  • TD Error (Temporal Difference): This is the "Surprise". If the TD error is positive, the outcome was better than expected. If negative, it was worse.
  • Off-Policy: Q-learning calculates the target using max Q(S',a'), assuming the agent will act optimally in the future, regardless of its current exploration policy.
