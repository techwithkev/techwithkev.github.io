What is Data Bias?

                

                    Data bias occurs when the data collected to train a system is not representative of the real-world population.
                

                

                    In this explorer, shifting the subgroup scores simulates sampling from a biased demographic (e.g. testing only in well-funded schools or ignoring minority regions), dragging down or bloating the statistics.
                

            


            
            

                
02

                
Variance & Mean Math

                

                    The combined mean $\mu_C$ is the weighted sum of Group A and Group B:
                    

                    Mean = (sum_A + sum_B) / (N_A + N_B)
                

                

                    Combined variance balloons exponentially as the subgroups move apart! 
                    This represents statistical spread: when subgroups are highly polarized, variance reflects group differences rather than natural individual variation.

How AI Uses This

                

                    If an AI model (like a neural network predictor) is trained on this combined dataset, the skewed overall statistics will severely impair its fairness.
                

                

                    The model will over-predict or under-predict for specific groups, leading to algorithmic discrimination in critical systems like credit scoring, admissions, or hiring filters.
