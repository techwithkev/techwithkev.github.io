THE KERNEL TRICK

Implicit Feature Mapping to 3D Space

Interactive Concept

The Concept

In 2D (slider at 0%), the red inner points and blue outer points cannot be separated by a single straight line. This means a standard linear classifier will fail.

By implicitly mapping them into a 3D space using the mathematical feature map:

Φ(x,y) = (x, y, x² + y²)

Notice how a flat 2D plane (the green grid) can now easily slide beneath the blue points and above the red points to perfectly separate the classes!
