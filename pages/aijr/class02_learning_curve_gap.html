High Bias (Underfitting)

                

                    At low complexity (left side of chart), the model lacks the capacity to capture structure. 
                    Both training and validation errors are extremely high.
                

                

                    This is called **High Bias**. The model makes simplistic assumptions, failing to learn patterns even on the training data itself.
                

            


            
            

                
02

                
High Variance (Overfitting)

                

                    At high complexity (right side), the model has massive memory capacity. It starts learning random fluctuations instead of the true pattern.
                

                

                    Training error drops to near-zero as it memorizes details, but **validation error shoots up** on unseen test data. The resulting **"Variance Gap"** reveals severe overfitting.
                

            


            
            

                
03

                
The Golden Sweetspot

                

                    The optimal model exists at the **valley** of the validation error curve. Here, complexity is perfectly balanced.
                

                

                    Notice how **adding data noise shifts the sweetspot left**! The dirtier the data, the easier it is to overfit, forcing AI developers to use simpler models or stricter regularization constraints.
