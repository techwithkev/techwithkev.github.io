Min-Max Normalization

                

                    Min-Max scales features strictly into a fixed boundary, typically **[0, 1]**. 
                    It is excellent when you have bounded ranges and no outliers (e.g. image pixel intensities 0–255).
                

                

                    ⚠️ Outlier Warning: Notice how dragging the red outlier to extreme coordinates forces all the other green dots to bunch together into a tiny, unreadable corner!
                

            


            
            

                
02

                
Z-Score Standardization

                

                    Z-Score centers data around a mean of **0** with a standard deviation of **1** ($\sigma$). 
                    It makes variables dimensionless and robust.
                

                

                    💪 Outlier Robustness: As you slide or drag the outlier, watch the standard deviation adjust. 
                    The rest of the cluster remains spread out and readable because scaling depends on variance, not absolute min/max!
                

            


            
            

                
03

                
Why Scale Features?

                

                    Most AI models calculate distances (like KNN or SVM) or run gradient descent optimization (like neural networks).
                

                

                    Without scaling, features with massive raw ranges (Income: $0–150k) completely swamp smaller ranges (Age: 0–100), making the AI ignore the smaller dimension entirely!
