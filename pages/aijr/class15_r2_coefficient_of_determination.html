R² COEFFICIENT OF DETERMINATION

Measuring the proportion of Variance Explained by your model

Interactive Concept

The Concept

R² = 1 - (SS_res / SS_tot)
  • SS_res (Residual Sum of Squares): The error of your model's predictions.
  • SS_tot (Total Sum of Squares): The inherent spread of the data around its mean.
