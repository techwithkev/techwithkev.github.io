The Concept

In a Transformer, every word asks: "Which other words should I pay attention to?"

The model computes attention weights — numbers between 0 and 1 that say how much each word focuses on every other word. Thicker lines = higher attention.

▶ How it computes this
1
Q Each token projects a Query: "What context am I looking for?"
2
K Every other token has a Key: "Here's what I represent."
3
Compute Q · K dot products — a high score means strong alignment.
4
Scale by ÷ √d to prevent values from exploding in high dimensions.
5
softmax(·) converts scores → probabilities that sum to 1. These are the line widths you see.
6
Output = weighted sum of V (Value) vectors. The token's new meaning is a blend of its context.
