The Concept

A Generative Model (like a VAE or GAN) learns to compress images into a compact latent vector — a point in a high-dimensional space.

Because this space is continuous and structured, you can move a point between two latent codes and decode each position to get a smooth visual morph.

▶ How the math works
1
The model encodes Image A → z_A and Image B → z_B, two vectors in latent space.
2
Pick a parameter t ∈ [0,1] along the path.
3
Compute: z(t) = (1−t)·z_A + t·z_B
4
Decode z(t) through the generator/decoder to get the interpolated image.
5
Sweeping t from 0→1 gives a smooth visual morph because the latent space is continuous.
