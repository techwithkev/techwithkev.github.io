What is the "Gradient"?

                

                    Think of the gradient as the steepness of the hill at the ball's location. If the hill is steep, the gradient is large. A positive gradient means the hill slopes upwards to the right, so we must go left (subtract) to head down the valley.
                

            


            
            

                
02

                
The Learning Rate (α)

                

                    The learning rate determines step size. If it's too small, the ball takes forever to reach the bottom (slow training). If it's too large, the ball overshoots the valley and can bounce higher and higher on the other side (divergence).
                

            


            
            

                
03

                
How AI Uses This

                

                    Large AI models (like ChatGPT or image generators) have billions of weights instead of just one. They calculate the gradient across a massive multi-dimensional valley and use this exact formula (called backpropagation) to learn!
