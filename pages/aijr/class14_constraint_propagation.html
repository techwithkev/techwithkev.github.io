The Concept

In a Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP), each variable has a domain of possible values. Adjacent map regions cannot share the same color.

AC-3 (Arc Consistency 3) propagates constraints: the moment you assign a color, that color is immediately struck from the domains of every neighboring region — forward checking in action.
