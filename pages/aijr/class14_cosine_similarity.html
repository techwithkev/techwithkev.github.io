COSINE SIMILARITY

Measuring distance in multi-dimensional vector spaces

Interactive Concept

The Concept

Recommender systems treat user tastes as a Vector pointing into a multi-dimensional space.

To find out how similar two users are, we don't look at the length of their arrows—we look at the Angle (θ) between them. A smaller angle means they point in the same direction!
