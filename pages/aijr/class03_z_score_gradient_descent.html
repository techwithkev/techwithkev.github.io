Why Z-Score Before Gradient Descent

Witness how feature scaling transforms an elongated, zigzagging loss bowl into a fast, circular convergence path.

01

Why It Zigzags

Imagine one axis is Income (0–$150,000) and the other is Age (0–100). Income is 1,500× bigger! The optimizer is extremely sensitive to the Income direction and barely moves in the Age direction.

⚠️ Result: It overshoots on the steep side and undershoots on the flat side — bouncing back and forth instead of going straight to the goal.

02

Why Z-Score Fixes It

Z-score rescales every feature so they all have the same average (0) and the same spread. Income and Age now live on the same scale — the bowl becomes a perfect circle.

💪 Result: Each step of gradient descent points straight at the goal. Fewer steps, no zigzag, and it always converges reliably.

03

The Real-World Impact

Every time you train a neural network or linear model in real life, you run gradient descent thousands of times. Without scaling, those zigzags waste huge amounts of compute time and can even prevent the model from learning at all.
